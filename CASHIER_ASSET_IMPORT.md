## Source inspection

### Checkout counter FBX

- Binary FBX version 7500, created with `FBX SDK/FBX Plugins version 2020.0.1` and originally saved by Autodesk 3ds Max 2021.
- Embedded creation timestamp: 2021-07-18 14:27:06.304; original metadata path: `G:\CGTrader\Checkout-kaspian\Checkout Counter.fbx`. This path is metadata only and does not establish a creator or license.
- Current axis metadata is Y-up; original up-axis metadata is Z-up. Unit scale factor is 1.0 cm. FBX2glTF emitted metre-scale glTF bounds.
- Two mesh objects (`checkout desk 1`, `checkout desk 2`), two materials, vertex positions/normals/colors/UVs, and 14,599 triangles.
- There are no `Texture` or `Video` objects, embedded images, external image paths, or image files beside the source. The conversion therefore has zero textures by design; no texture was missing and none was invented.
- The only animation take, `Take 001`, has zero channels. FBX2glTF reported and skipped it rather than writing an empty animation.
- Initial GLB bounds: min `(-1.01173, 0.04085, 0.14975)`, max `(1.18866, 1.37038, 0.97348)`. Dimensions are `2.20039 × 1.32953 × 0.82373` m. The authored origin is offset from the bounds centre; runtime placement recentres X/Z and grounds minimum Y without modifying the file.

### Cash register GLB from ZIP

- Binary glTF 2.0; two meshes, three mesh primitives, two materials, positions/normals/UVs, 9,927 triangles, and no animation.
- Six embedded JPEG maps at 2048×2048: diffuse, normal, and metallic/roughness maps for the opaque body and glass material. There are no external dependencies or missing images.
- Bounds: min `(-0.22518, 0.00218, -0.17349)`, max `(0.37531, 0.62354, 0.45071)`. Dimensions are `0.60049 × 0.62136 × 0.62420` m. The origin is asymmetric in X/Z and approximately grounded in Y.
- The source does not include tangent attributes while using normal maps. The Khronos validator therefore reports three generated-tangent-space portability warnings. MikkTSpace tangents were generated for the final asset. Three UV-degenerate vertices initially produced zero-length tangents; each received a stable unit fallback orthogonal to its normal. This changes only the undefined tangent basis at those degenerate UV points. The final asset validates cleanly and Three.js rendered the normal-mapped materials correctly.

### Shopping basket GLB

- Binary glTF 2.0 generated by `Microsoft GLTF Exporter 2.4.2.20`.
- One mesh/primitive, one double-sided masked material, positions/normals/UVs/vertex colors, 496 triangles, and no animation.
- Source bounds: min `(-0.256, -0.20428, 0.22453)`, max `(0.256, 0.20428, 0.28747)`. Dimensions are `0.51200 × 0.40856 × 0.06294` m. The thin Z extent is authored geometry, not a conversion error; runtime inspection confirmed the basket silhouette is upright at human scale.
- Four embedded 8×8 PNG images provide redundant solid-color PBR/specular-gloss values. There are no external dependencies or missing images. The source uses optional `KHR_materials_pbrSpecularGlossiness` but does not require an extension.
- The origin is centred in X/Y and offset in Z. Runtime placement recentres X/Z and grounds minimum Y.

## Conversion and conservative optimization

### Checkout

1. FBX2glTF 0.9.7 converted the exact incoming FBX to a temporary binary glTF 2.0 file.
2. glTF Transform applied `dedup`, bitwise-safe `weld`, and `prune`.
3. Unused UV data was pruned because the FBX contains no texture dependency.
4. Geometry was **not simplified**. Triangle count, mesh separation, vertex colors, normals, materials, dimensions, and silhouette remain intact.

### Register

1. The required source GLB was extracted outside the repository; the ZIP was not edited.
2. Its six embedded JPEG maps were resized from 2048² to 512², preserving all six material channels.
3. `dedup`, bitwise-safe `weld`, and `prune` removed only redundant/unused resources. Geometry was not simplified.
4. MikkTSpace tangent attributes were added for portable normal mapping. Stable orthogonal fallbacks repaired the three undefined results at UV-degenerate vertices; triangle topology, positions, normals, UVs, materials, and dimensions are unchanged.

### Basket

1. The deprecated specular/glossiness data and four redundant solid 8×8 textures were removed while equivalent material factors and vertex colors were retained.
2. The now-unused UV accessor was removed.
3. Geometry, triangle count, one material, alpha mode, double-sided state, dimensions, normals, and vertex colors were preserved. No simplification was applied.
